Before You Go

🌞 Best Season

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Dinagat Islands is from March to May (dry season), when the weather is sunny and seas are calm — ideal for beach activities and island hopping. The island experiences typhoons from July to November, so it's best to avoid travel during this period. Check the schedule for the Kinabog, Lawigan, and Sirong Sirong festivals — celebrated under the spirit of Bugkosa sa Dinagat — for an unforgettable cultural experience alongside your island visit.

💡 Travel Tips

Tips for Travelers

  • Bring cash — ATM availability is limited; there is one BDO branch in San Jose.
  • Join one of the local festivals (Kinabog, Lawigan, Sirong Sirong) for an authentic and immersive Dinagateño cultural experience.
  • Book ferry tickets in advance during peak season and holidays.
  • Hire a local guide for jungle treks — they know the safest and most scenic paths.
  • Bring reef-safe sunscreen to protect the island's coral ecosystems.
  • Buy from local vendors to directly support the island community.
