Taste & Tradition

Food & Culture

Taste the soul of Dinagat — a rich tapestry of indigenous traditions, Spanish heritage, and ocean-kissed cuisine.

Local Delicacies

Dinagat's cuisine is shaped by the sea's abundance and the land's tropical richness. Here are the must-try dishes and drinks of the island.

Seafood

Kinilaw na Tuna

Fresh raw tuna marinated in vinegar, coconut milk, chili, and ginger. A beloved Surigaonon staple that showcases the island's finest catch.

Grilled & Raw

Sinuglaw

A beloved combination of grilled pork (sinugba) and kinilaw — a flavor-packed local fusion dish that represents the best of Dinagat's culinary creativity.

Sweet Soup

Binignit

A warm sweet soup made of glutinous rice, coconut milk, bananas, taro, and sweet potato. Traditionally served during Holy Week, it warms both body and soul.

Rice Cake

Biko

Sticky rice cake cooked in coconut milk and brown sugar, often topped with latik (coconut curds). A classic Filipino kakanin found at every celebration in Dinagat.

Fish Soup

Tinolang Isda

A light, ginger-based fish soup flavored with green papaya and moringa leaves, made with the freshest catch from local waters — simple, nourishing, and delicious.

Traditional Drink

Coconut Wine (Tuba)

A mildly fermented coconut sap wine, a traditional drink deeply embedded in local social culture. Best enjoyed with friends while watching the sunset on the beach.

Festivals & Traditions

The spirit of Dinagat comes alive through its colorful celebrations. Plan your visit around these vibrant events for an unforgettable cultural experience.

✊

Bugkosa sa Dinagat

A celebration of unity and community spirit — "Bugkosa" means "to unite" — bringing together all the people of Dinagat Islands through shared traditions, music, and festivity. It is the spirit that ties together the island's major festivals.

🌈

Kinabog Festival

A vibrant festival showcasing the rich heritage and colorful traditions of Dinagat, celebrated with energetic street dancing, indigenous costumes, and cultural performances that draw visitors from across the region.

🌊

Lawigan Festival

A lively community festival honoring the island's deep connection to the sea and its roots. Features local music, indigenous rituals, a feast of traditional cuisine, and competitions that celebrate island life.

🎶

Sirong Sirong Festival

A joyful and colorful celebration named after the island's unique spirit and energy. Filled with street parades, traditional music, dance performances, and community gatherings that light up the island.

Cultural Heritage

Dinagat Islands is known as the "Mystical Province of Love." Its soul is woven from the threads of the indigenous Mamanwa people, Spanish colonial Catholicism, fishing traditions, and the unique spiritual movement of the PBMA. Visitors are welcomed not just as tourists, but as guests of a living, breathing community with centuries of story to tell. Come with an open heart, and Dinagat will give you memories you will carry for a lifetime.

— Dinagat Islands Tourism Office
