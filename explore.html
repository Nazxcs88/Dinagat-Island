Explore Dinagat

A guide to the island's natural wonders, hidden treasures, and cultural landmarks.

Must-See Attractions

From crystal-blue lagoons and pristine beaches to lush forest trails and peaceful island towns — Dinagat Islands offers experiences that stay with you long after you leave.

Blue Lagoon

A breathtaking stretch of electric-blue waters sheltered by lush green hills. One of Dinagat's most iconic natural wonders, perfect for swimming and photography.

Nature & Water

Cagdianao Boulevard

A scenic seaside promenade lined with coconut palms and ocean views, ideal for an evening stroll. The boulevard is the social heartbeat of Cagdianao town.

Town & Scenery

Bonsai Forest

A magical grove of naturally stunted trees that resemble giant bonsai, draped in moss and mist. A rare and otherworldly sight found only in Dinagat's highlands.

Nature & Trekking

Bitaog Beach

Fine white sand and crystal-clear waters, perfect for swimming and snorkeling. One of Dinagat's most accessible and beloved shores for both locals and visitors.

Beach

Miracle Pool Cave

A mystical cave hiding a serene natural pool within its chambers. Locals believe the waters hold healing properties — an almost sacred site on the island.

Cave & Mystery

Pagkawasan Garden Beach Resort

A lush garden resort nestled along a calm beachfront, offering a perfect blend of nature and relaxation. A favorite retreat for families and couples alike.

Resort & Beach

San Jose Town

The provincial capital and main gateway to Dinagat Islands. A vibrant town with a bustling port, local markets, and the warm hospitality of Dinagateño life.

Town & Culture

Tagbirayan Beach

A tranquil beach with powdery white sands and calm turquoise waters, tucked away from the crowds. The perfect spot for quiet reflection and undisturbed sunsets.

Beach

Cabacongan Beach

A scenic stretch of shoreline with clear waters and gentle waves, ideal for swimming, picnicking, and watching bangka boats glide past as the sun sets.

Beach

Duyos Beach

A secluded and serene beach surrounded by tropical greenery. The calm waters and natural shade make it a favorite for those seeking a quieter island escape.

Beach

Dinagat Roads

The scenic coastal and mountain roads of Dinagat offer some of the most breathtaking views on the island — a journey through paradise with every turn.

Scenic Drive

Jelmars Resort

A charming island resort known for its crystal waters, cozy accommodations, and warm island hospitality. A great base for exploring the surrounding islands.

Resort & Island Hopping
